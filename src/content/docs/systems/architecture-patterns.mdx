An index of the patterns that recur across every insurance platform, regardless of vendor or
market. Each of these deserves its own chapter; this page is the map.

<Ledger>
  <LedgerRow term="Effective dating">
    How a PAS versions products, coverages and policies over time. Out-of-sequence endorsements
    are the classic hard problem of insurance systems.
    <Eg>an address change effective 1 May arrives after the 1 June renewal was already processed — the system must reopen and re-rate both policy terms correctly.</Eg>
  </LedgerRow>
  <LedgerRow term="Rating architecture">
    Externalised rate tables actuaries maintain themselves, with rate versioning tied to
    regulatory filings.
    <Eg>a motor rate revision effective 1 April applies to new business and renewals incepting after that date — while mid-term endorsements on older policies keep the old rates.</Eg>
  </LedgerRow>
  <LedgerRow term="Integration surface">
    Broker and aggregator APIs, ACORD messages, KYC/AML, payment gateways, garage and hospital
    networks, government registries, reinsurance bordereaux.
    <Eg>a single motor quote journey may call the vehicle registry for RC details, a previous-insurer API for NCB verification, and a payment gateway — before the PAS ever issues.</Eg>
  </LedgerRow>
  <LedgerRow term="Long-running data">
    A life policy outlives multiple system generations, making data migration and closed-book
    administration perennial programmes.
    <Eg>an endowment sold in 1998 must still pay its maturity in 2028, bonus history intact, after three core-system migrations.</Eg>
  </LedgerRow>
  <LedgerRow term="Regulatory NFRs">
    Audit trails, data residency and retention, solvency and statistical reporting, IFRS 17 feeds.
  </LedgerRow>
</Ledger>
