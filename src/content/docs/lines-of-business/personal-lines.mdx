<Accordion title="Motor / Auto Insurance" tag="Motor">
The largest general line in most markets, including India. Two components: **third-party
liability** (injury or damage you cause others — legally mandatory in India under the Motor
Vehicles Act) and **own damage** (your vehicle — accident, theft, fire, flood).

Pricing uses vehicle make and model, geography, and claims history; the **No Claim Bonus** is
the big retention lever. Claims run through garage networks, cashless repair, surveyors and
depreciation rules. High volume and thin margins make this the line where straight-through
processing matters most.

<Example>
A comprehensive policy on a recent hatchback with 20% No Claim Bonus. Monsoon floods seize the
engine — payable only because the owner bought the engine-protection add-on; standard
own-damage cover excludes hydrostatic lock.
</Example>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Home / Householders / Personal Property" tag="Home">
Covers building structure and/or contents against fire, natural catastrophe and burglary, often
with liability add-ons. Sum insured is based on **reconstruction cost, not market value** —
underinsurance is chronic. Catastrophe exposure makes reinsurance and accumulation management
critical.

<Example>
A flat insured for ₹60 lakh reconstruction cost plus ₹8 lakh contents. A burglary claim for
jewellery pays out — but only up to the jewellery sub-limit, a detail most customers discover
at claim time. *(Illustrative figures.)*
</Example>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Travel Insurance" tag="Travel">
Short-duration cover for trip cancellation, overseas medical emergencies, baggage and delays.
Almost entirely digital and **embedded** — sold with tickets and visas. High volume, low
premium, automated claims: a favourite line for insurtech and API-first distribution.

<Example>
A flight lands eight hours late; the insurer detects it from airline data and auto-pays the
fixed delay benefit before the traveller files a claim — near-parametric claims handling.
</Example>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Personal Accident" tag="PA">
Fixed benefits for accidental death or disability — lump sums or weekly benefits. Simple
product, widely bundled and sold through banks and employers.
</Accordion>
