Covers medical expenses: hospitalisation, day-care procedures, pre- and post-hospitalisation,
and increasingly OPD and wellness.

Two payout models: **indemnity** (reimburse actual bills — the family-floater mediclaim) and
**fixed benefit** (a lump sum on diagnosis — critical illness, hospital cash).

The mechanics that matter for systems: network hospitals and **cashless claims** settled
directly with hospitals via TPA pre-authorisation, waiting periods for pre-existing diseases,
sub-limits, co-pays and cumulative bonuses.

<Example>
A ₹10 lakh family floater: an appendectomy at a network hospital is pre-authorised by the TPA
and settled cashless at ₹1.2 lakh. The same member's planned knee surgery in year one is
declined — pre-existing condition, still inside the waiting period. *(Illustrative figures.)*
</Example>

:::caution[Different markets, different domains]
**US health insurance is a different universe** — payers, providers, HIPAA, X12 EDI claims.
Treat it as its own domain rather than assuming anything on this page transfers.
:::
