<Accordion title="Commercial Property / Fire & Business Interruption" tag="Property">
Business buildings, machinery and stock against fire and allied perils. Larger risks get
risk-engineering surveys. **Business Interruption** — loss of profit while operations are down
after damage — is the sophisticated companion. Mega risks involve co-insurance across multiple
insurers and heavy facultative reinsurance.

<Example>
A factory fire halts production for four months. The property section rebuilds the plant;
Business Interruption separately pays the lost gross profit and ongoing salaries — often the
larger cheque of the two.
</Example>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Liability: CGL, PI/E&O, D&O, Workers' Comp" tag="Liability">
Legal liability to third parties. **CGL**: injury or damage from operations or premises.
**Professional Indemnity / E&O**: negligence by professionals. **D&O**: protects directors
against shareholder and regulatory claims. **Workers' Compensation / Employer's Liability**:
workplace injuries.

Liability is **long-tail** — claims can surface and settle years later, which drives complex
reserving and makes the accident-year versus reporting-year distinction load-bearing.

<Example>
A coding defect corrupts a client's billing data — the E&O policy defends and settles. A
customer slips on a wet floor in a store — that's a CGL claim. A shareholder sues the board
over a disclosure — D&O responds.
</Example>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Marine Cargo & Hull" tag="Marine">
The oldest line in existence. **Cargo** covers goods in transit by sea, air, road or rail, under
the Institute Cargo Clauses (A/B/C). **Hull** covers the vessels. Certificate issuance for
individual shipments under open covers is a classic system workflow.

<Example>
An electronics importer holds an annual open cover; every shipment auto-generates a marine
certificate. One container is lost overboard in a storm — a straightforward ICC(A) all-risks
cargo claim.
</Example>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Engineering: CAR / EAR / Machinery" tag="Engg">
Construction and machinery covers: **Contractor's All Risks** and **Erection All Risks** for
projects, plus Machinery Breakdown and Contractor's Plant & Machinery. Policies often run
multi-year to match project timelines — which breaks any system assuming an annual term.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Cyber Insurance" tag="Cyber">
The fastest-growing commercial line worldwide: breach response costs, ransomware recovery,
business interruption from outages, and liability for leaked data. Underwriting assesses the
client's security posture, sometimes via automated external scans. Wordings and pricing evolve
rapidly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Crop / Agriculture" tag="Crop">
In India, dominated by government-subsidised, yield-based schemes. Loss assessment uses weather
stations, satellite imagery and crop-cutting experiments. **Parametric** versions, where a
rainfall index triggers automatic payout, are an active innovation area.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Specialty Lines" tag="Specialty">
Aviation, energy, trade credit, surety bonds, political risk, fine art, event cancellation,
kidnap and ransom. Low volume, high expertise, London-market flavoured.
</Accordion>
