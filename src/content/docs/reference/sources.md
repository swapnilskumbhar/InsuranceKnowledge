Stable starting points. Regulators and standards bodies come first: they are free, they are
authoritative, and they are the citation a factual claim in this handbook should point at.

## Regulators and standards

| Source | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| [IRDAI](https://irdai.gov.in) | The Indian regulator. The Annual Report and circulars are a free market education. |
| [NAIC](https://www.naic.org) | US regulators' association — glossary, consumer guides, market data. |
| [ACORD](https://www.acord.org) | The data-exchange standards you meet in every broker–carrier integration. |
| [Lloyd's of London](https://www.lloyds.com) | How the specialty marketplace works; market reports and syndicate results. |

## On using these

The handbook's editorial rule is that every factual claim carries a source in the page's
`sources` frontmatter. Most pages currently say *"Author synthesis — REQUIRES REVIEW"*, which is
honest but is not a source. Replacing those with entries from this list — or better, with the
specific circular or wording being relied on — is a prerequisite for publication.
