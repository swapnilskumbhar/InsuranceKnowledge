<Ledger>
  <LedgerRow term="Underwriter">
    Evaluates submissions and decides accept/decline, price and terms. Largely automated in
    personal lines; judgment-heavy in commercial. Lives in the PAS and the underwriting workbench.
  </LedgerRow>
  <LedgerRow term="Actuary">
    Pricing actuaries build rating models; reserving actuaries estimate future claim costs;
    others do capital modelling and filings. Massive consumers of core-system data.
  </LedgerRow>
  <LedgerRow term="Broker / Agent (producer)">
    Sells and services policies — "producer" is the generic term. Their agency management systems
    and portals are a large carrier integration surface.
  </LedgerRow>
  <LedgerRow term="Claims adjuster / handler">
    From FNOL to settlement: verify coverage, investigate, set reserves, negotiate, pay. Field
    adjusters inspect; desk adjusters work files remotely.
  </LedgerRow>
  <LedgerRow term="Risk engineer">
    Inspects factories and commercial properties and recommends improvements; reports feed
    underwriting decisions.
  </LedgerRow>
  <LedgerRow term="SIU / fraud investigator">
    Special Investigation Unit — fraud detection and investigation. Fraud analytics is a
    recurring software project.
  </LedgerRow>
  <LedgerRow term="Product manager">
    Designs products: coverage structure, wording, pricing framework, regulatory filing. In
    system terms, product configuration in the PAS.
  </LedgerRow>
  <LedgerRow term="Operations / policy servicing">
    Back-office issuance, endorsements, renewals, cancellations and correspondence — the daily
    users of your workflows.
  </LedgerRow>
  <LedgerRow term="Compliance & legal">
    Keep products, sales and claims within regulation. Drive audit trail, retention and
    reporting requirements.
  </LedgerRow>
  <LedgerRow term="Ceded reinsurance analyst">
    Manages treaties, cessions and recoveries — often on legacy or manual tooling.
  </LedgerRow>
  <LedgerRow term="CAT modeller / exposure manager">
    Tracks risk accumulation — total exposure in one earthquake zone, one port, one cyclone path
    — using catastrophe models.
  </LedgerRow>
  <LedgerRow term="TPA claims processor">
    Runs cashless pre-authorisations and reimbursement adjudication in health.
  </LedgerRow>
</Ledger>

<SystemsNote>
These roles are **not** a permission matrix, and modelling them as one is the mistake to avoid.
The same human is an underwriter on one submission and a referral approver on another; a claims
handler's authority is bounded by reserve amount, not by job title.

Model **authority limits as data attached to the transaction type and amount**, with role as
one input among several. Systems that hardcode "underwriter can approve" need a release every
time the business changes a threshold.
</SystemsNote>
