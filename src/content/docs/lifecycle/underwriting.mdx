## Bind is the event that matters

Coverage begins at **bind**, not at document issue. This sounds like a detail and is not: it
means a policy can be legally in force while its documents are still generating, and a claim
can arrive in that window.

Systems that treat issuance as the state transition — rather than bind — cannot answer whether
a loss on the day of sale is covered.
