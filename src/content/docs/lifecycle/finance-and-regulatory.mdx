## Data lineage is a functional requirement

This is the layer where "the system produces the right answer" stops being sufficient. A
solvency return or an IFRS 17 disclosure must be **defensible**: you have to show which
transactions produced a figure, under which product and rate versions, and reproduce it months
later after the underlying policies have moved on.

That requirement propagates backwards into every system feeding it. It is far cheaper to design
for than to retrofit.
