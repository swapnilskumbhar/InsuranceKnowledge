## One product, many channels

The same product sold through three channels is three different systems problems: different
commission structures, different data captured at proposal, different regulatory disclosure
obligations, and different expectations about how fast a quote must return.

Aggregators in particular invert the usual assumption — you are one of several quotes rendered
side by side, and a slow response is functionally a declined risk.

<Gaps>
- No treatment of what a quote actually is as a domain object: how long it lives, whether it is
  binding, and what happens when the rate version changes between quote and bind.
- Commission structures are named but not modelled.
- Nothing on aggregator integration patterns or the quote-journey timing budget.
</Gaps>
