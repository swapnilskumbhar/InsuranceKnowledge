## Why this is a release-management problem

A product is not a configuration record. It is a **versioned artefact with a regulatory
lifecycle**: drafted, filed, approved, effective from a date, superseded, and retained for as
long as any policy written on it is still live or still capable of producing a claim.

That means a product version outlives the policies written on it, and two policies sold a day
apart may be on different versions with different wordings and different rates — permanently.
