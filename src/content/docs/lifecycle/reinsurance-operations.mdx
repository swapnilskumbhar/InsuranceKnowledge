## Treaty and facultative

**Treaty** reinsurance is automatic and portfolio-wide: agreed in advance, applied to every
risk meeting the criteria. **Facultative** is negotiated per individual risk. Proportional
structures (quota share, surplus) share premium and losses by a fixed proportion; non-
proportional (excess of loss) responds only above an attachment point.

<Example>
An insurer accepts a ₹50 crore factory fire risk but wants only ₹5 crore on its own books: it
retains ₹5 crore and cedes ₹45 crore under its surplus treaty. Separately, its catastrophe
excess-of-loss treaty pays whenever a single event costs the insurer more than ₹20 crore in
total claims. *(Illustrative figures.)*
</Example>
