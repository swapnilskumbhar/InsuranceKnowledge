## Why this is a separate module

Billing looks like a subset of policy administration and behaves like a different domain. A
policy transaction produces a premium *amount*; billing decides **who owes what, when, and what
happens when they don't pay** — which involves installment schedules, partial payments,
suspense accounts, refunds that cross policy terms, and commission clawbacks.

The major platforms ship billing separately for a reason, and homegrown systems that fold it
into the PAS tend to discover why during their first cancellation-for-non-payment cycle.
